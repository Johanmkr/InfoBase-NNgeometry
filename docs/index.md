# Neural Network Geometry

A textbook and literature review built from a Zotero collection of research
papers. Every claim links to the paper it comes from.

- **[Textbook](textbook/index.md)**: the material of the whole collection in teaching order, with exercises.
- **[Comparison](comparison/index.md)**: concept map, timeline, comparison tables and themes across papers.
- **[Papers](papers/index.md)**: one structured summary per paper.
- **[Notation](notation.md)**: the shared notation used throughout, mapped to each paper's symbols.
- **[References](references.md)**: the bibliography.

!!! info "How this is made"
    The pages are generated by AI agents from the papers themselves and checked
    for citation consistency. Treat them as a study aid: the cited papers are
    the authority.
